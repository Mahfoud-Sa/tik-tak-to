Game Capabilities

Built for Seamless Play

Highlights of the features implemented in the XO Game project across web and standalone editions.

Instant 3x3 Grid

Responsive canvas with immediate win detection across rows, columns, and diagonals, plus draw detection and score tracking.

10 Color Themes

Customizable aesthetic color themes inspired by the desktop Tkinter palette, allowing players to personalize grid and token hues.

LAN & Local Multiplayer

Socket-based architecture supporting direct client-server connections over local networks, as well as local pass-and-play matches.

Auto-Update System

Built-in background update engine with SemVer comparison, release notes inspection, and direct Windows package installation.

Rules & Controls

How to Play

Simple rules for players of all ages. Keyboard and touch-friendly.

1

Take Turns Marking

Player X always moves first. Click, tap, or use arrow keys with Enter/Space (or keys 1–9) to claim an empty grid cell.

2

Form 3 in a Row

Align three of your marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally before your opponent does to win the round.

3

Block & Strategize

Defend your lines by anticipating your opponent’s next move. If all 9 cells are filled without a 3-in-a-row, it’s a draw!
